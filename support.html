Support

Questions or feedback about a build? Email support@keyzzo.com. Include your device model and iOS version if something is misbehaving.

Using the app

How do I switch between Wide and Duet?

Use the Wide / Duet control in the top bar. In face-to-face Duet on a regular iPhone, that control sits in the bar between the two keyboards, so neither player has upside-down chrome.

How do octaves work?

Each keyboard has an Octave control, from C1 to C6. Wide Mode opens on C3. In Duet Mode the top or Left keyboard opens on C4, and the bottom or Right keyboard opens on C3. The two sides are independent.

Where are Sustain and note labels?

Sustain is a button in the same bar as the mode switch. Note Labels and Show Octave are in the ••• menu. All Notes Off is there too, if a note gets stuck.

Does Keyzzo need the internet?

No. The synth runs on the device, and the app is designed to work offline.

Does it collect data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
