Keyzzo

A piano that spans both screens.

On iPhone Duo, Wide Mode runs one keyboard across the fold. Duet Mode gives each person their own keys. On a regular iPhone the same two modes stack: a single keyboard, or two players facing each other.

For iPhone Duo and iPhone. Sound comes from a built-in synth. It works offline, and it does not collect data.

Two players, one phone.

In Duet Mode each person gets a keyboard and their own octave control. On a regular iPhone they sit top and bottom, with the mode switch and Sustain in a bar between them. On an open Duo they sit Left and Right, both upright, 15 white keys each.

The same keyboard, one screen.

Wide Mode on a regular iPhone is a single keyboard you can scroll and zoom, starting at a C you choose. It opens on C3. Octave range is C1 through C6.

What’s in the app

Taken from the current build. Nothing more.

  • Wide Mode

    On an open Duo, 28 white keys run across both screens. On a regular iPhone you get 21, and you can scroll and zoom the keyboard.

  • Duet Mode

    Two independent keyboards. Left and Right on Duo (15 white keys). Player A and Player B on iPhone (12 white keys), with the top half turned to face the other person.

  • Touch

    A multitouch keyboard: chords are several fingers at once, and sliding from one key to the next changes the note. Sustain is a button in the bar. Note labels, with or without the octave number, live in the ••• menu, along with All Notes Off.

  • Sound

    Notes play through a built-in, low-latency synth on the device. There is no account and no network requirement.

  • No data collected

    The app does not collect, store, or share personal data. See the Privacy Policy.
