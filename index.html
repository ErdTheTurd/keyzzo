Built for iPhone Duo & iPhone

Two screens. One expressive piano.

Keyzzo turns your iPhone Duo into a wide multi-octave keyboard or a face-to-face duet piano—with low-latency audio that works completely offline.

Designed around how you play

Wide Mode

Stretch a continuous multi-octave keyboard across both Duo screens, or scroll and zoom smoothly on a single-screen iPhone.

Duet Mode

Each player gets their own keyboard facing them, with independent octave controls—split across both Duo screens or top-and-bottom on iPhone.

Low-Latency & Offline

Responsive multitouch chords, glissando, sustain, and note labels powered by an on-device audio engine with no internet connection required.
