Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy describes the Keyzzo iPhone app and this website. Keyzzo is made by Edward Dunn.

No account

Keyzzo does not require an account. There is no sign-in, and there is no cloud account to create.

What the app does on your device

Keyzzo is a piano. Touch on the keyboard is turned into sound by a built-in synth that runs on the device. The app is designed to work offline. It is not designed to send your playing, settings, or any personal data to Edward Dunn or to anyone else.

No data collected

The app does not collect, store, track, or share personal data, analytics, or audio. This build does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not sell your data.

This website

This site is a static marketing site. It does not collect form data. There is no contact form that posts information to a server.

Like most websites, your browser may send standard technical requests (such as your IP address) to the host that serves these pages. That is ordinary web hosting behavior; this site itself does not add tracking pixels or analytics scripts.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@keyzzo.com.

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
